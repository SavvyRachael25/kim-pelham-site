The Cascade Model

One blog post becomes 30+ days of social content.

This playbook shows how each of Kim's 4 cornerstone blog posts transforms into a complete social media cascade across Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn. The goal is consistent, strategic visibility without oversaturation—delivering value at every touchpoint while building authority and trust.

How It Works

Each blog launches with a single announcement post, then unfolds over 28 days through carefully spaced content: statistics, behind-the-scenes insights, client stories, neighborhood context, and thought leadership. This approach keeps your name in front of your audience while giving each piece of content room to breathe.

Platforms

Facebook, Instagram (feed posts, Stories, Reels), and LinkedIn. Content is adapted for each platform's native strengths.

Frequency

5–7 posts per week across all platforms combined. Never more than one post per platform per day.

Voice & Tone

Warm, authoritative, personal. Equal blend of hard data and heart. Kim's unique perspective—no fluff, all substance.
