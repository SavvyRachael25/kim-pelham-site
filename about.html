From Local to Expert: Kim's Real Estate Journey

With 14 years of real estate experience — plus three years in commercial property management — Kim Pelham has built her reputation on one simple principle: every real estate journey is deeply personal. Kim has called the Pacific Northwest home at different chapters of her life — from childhood through second grade, then again in Bothell from 1996 to 2002, and now in Everett since 2015, and these communities aren't just markets to her—they're home.

Kim is a Pacific Northwest broker who brings data-driven expertise paired with genuine care for her clients' goals. She knows these communities inside and out—from the vibrant neighborhoods of Everett to the tree-lined streets of Mill Creek, from the historic charm of Snohomish to the dynamic growth of Bothell. Her deep roots run through every neighborhood she serves.
