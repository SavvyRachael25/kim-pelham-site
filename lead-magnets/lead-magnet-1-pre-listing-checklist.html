Your Pre-Listing Launch Checklist

Master the critical 72 hours that shape your home sale

The first three days on the market aren't just the beginning—they're frequently the deciding factor in how quickly your home sells and what price it commands. This checklist guides you through everything that happens before your home goes live, so you can launch with confidence and momentum.

Two Weeks Before Launch

Strategic Repairs & Inspections
Schedule home inspection to identify critical repairs
Address foundation, roof, HVAC, plumbing issues
Fix items that "scare buyers" (structural, electrical, water)
Skip cosmetic fixes—focus on friction-point removers
Preparation & Staging Planning
Book professional staging consultant for consultation
Begin decluttering—remove personal items and excess furniture
Deep clean every room (windows, baseboards, carpets)
Ensure neutral paint colors (refresh if needed)

One Week Before Launch

Professional Staging & Photography Setup
Complete full staging (furniture arrangement, lighting, flow)
Schedule professional photography (natural light hours)
Take interior & exterior photos (daylight + dusk photos)
Plan drone photography if property warrants
Marketing & Online Presence
Finalize listing description and key features
Prepare social media content calendar
Schedule Facebook/Instagram ad campaigns
Draft email marketing to past buyers

Day Before Launch

Final walkthrough—check every room for perfection
Confirm all listing information is accurate
Prepare scheduling system for showing requests
Plan your weekend away (let momentum work without you)
