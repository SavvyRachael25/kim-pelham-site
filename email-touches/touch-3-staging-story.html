Tale of Two Brokers

The situation: 2001 built home in Edmonds. Three bedrooms. Great bones. But it had been on the market for 2 months with a different broker. Zero offers. Zero showings in the last 30 days.

The problem: It was the home itself. It was the presentation. Dated photos. No staging. Seller's furniture still there. Buyers were driving by a project, not a home.

The solution: We pulled the listing. Worked with the seller on staging. Professional photography. Virtual tour. Relisted at the same price.

The result: Sold in 5 days. $10,000 over asking. Multiple offers.
