## 1. Current Mortgage Rate Trends

### Current Rates (as of March 27, 2026)
- **30-year fixed rate:** 6.37%
- **15-year fixed rate:** 5.87%
- **Trend:** Rates rose in March after steady decreases in Q4 2025 and early 2026

### Key Insights
- **Status vs. 6%:** Rates are currently **above 6%** and trending upward
- **YoY Comparison:** Rates averaging 6.18% for Jan-Feb 2026 vs. above 7% same period 2025
- **Contributing Factors:** Rising unemployment, stagnant inflation reduction, Federal Reserve pause on rate cuts
- **2026 Outlook:** Expected to average 6.1% annually, with potential range of 5.7%-6.5%

### Forecast
- Many projections predict gradual rate decreases through 2026
- Short-term increases remain possible
- Rates are still significantly more favorable than 2024-2025 highs

- **Active listings (Jan 2026):** 12,376 properties
- **YoY growth:** +20.9% compared to January 2025

### Market Shift Significance
- **More than 300 additional listings** compared to January 2025
- Inventory has increased just enough to create choice without oversupply
- Market moving toward normalization after years of extremely tight supply
- Homes priced and presented well continue to sell

### Buyer/Seller Dynamics
- More choices for buyers
- Less competition for sellers than previous years
- Increased negotiating power for buyers
- Homes taking slightly longer to sell (market normalization, not crisis)

## 4. NAR (National Association of Realtors) News & Policy Changes

### Coming Soon Listings Policy
- NAR reaffirmed support for MLS transparency regarding "coming soon" listings
- **No national policy:** Local MLS organizations retain discretion on listing statuses
- Reflects industrywide shift toward flexibility in membership and data access requirements
- Many associations moving to offer more flexible membership options

### MLS and Membership Evolution
- Emphasis on local MLS discretion over strict national membership requirements
- Three-way agreement under review (not revoked but deemphasized)
- Industry leaders increasingly advocating for flexible membership options
- Data access becoming more negotiable at local level

### Legislative Advocacy
- NAR applauded Senate passage of the **21st Century ROAD to Housing Act**
- Focus on bipartisan housing supply crisis solutions
- Executive Vice President Shannon McGahn emphasized commitment to addressing housing shortage

### Litigation Update
- Louisiana District Court dismissed federal antitrust charges against NAR in March 2026
- Judge Shelly D. Dick granted motion to dismiss allegations regarding forced three-memberships

### Implication for Agents
- More flexibility in membership structure emerging
- Local MLS changes may offer new options for different agent profiles
- Legislative attention on supply supports long-term market stabilization efforts

## 6. SEO/AEO/GEO Trends in Real Estate Marketing (2026)

### Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) - Critical Shift
- **What it is:** Structuring content to directly answer questions for AI-powered search engines
- **Zero-click reality:** 60% of Google searches result in zero clicks
- **Key strategy:** Answer questions in first paragraph/first 200 characters or AI will skip your content
- **Impact:** AEO is reshaping how real estate pros attract leads vs. traditional ranked listings

### Traditional SEO Still Essential
- Google still drives massive traffic
- Ranking #1 is necessary but no longer sufficient
- Must optimize for both traditional search AND AI overview results
- Visibility on Google remains non-negotiable foundation

### Hyper-Local & GEO (Generative Experience Optimization)
- Move beyond city-wide searches to neighborhood/street-level targeting
- Tech-enabled, hyper-local strategy with authentic human connection
- Target specific neighborhoods, school districts, individual streets
- More effective than broad-spectrum advertising

### Content Strategy Changes
- Authenticity outperforms polish in video marketing
- Casual style more effective than overly produced commercials
- Modern viewers skeptical of traditional "salesy" approaches
- First 200 characters of any content (blog, video, listing description) critical for AI discovery

### Mobile & Voice Search Growing
- **72% of users** start search on mobile devices
- Voice search growth expected to accelerate
- Optimize for conversational queries like "homes for sale in Snohomish under $750K"
- Voice-friendly content now differentiates early movers

### Additional Considerations
- Listing descriptions need AEO optimization, not just pretty copy
- YouTube and blog content must answer questions upfront
- Local search increasingly neighborhood-specific rather than city-wide
- AI citation visibility becoming as important as click-through rates

- **Timeline:** Construction expected to begin in 2026
- **Impact:** Enhanced Portland-Vancouver connectivity, reduced commute times, increased attractiveness to workers

#### Light Rail & High-Speed Rail Expansion
- Portland light rail system expansion underway
- Planned high-speed rail connections between major PNW cities
- Enhancing attractiveness for residents and investors across Oregon and Washington

#### North Spokane Corridor (NSC)
- Completion will dramatically improve connectivity
- Expected to unlock previously difficult-to-access development areas
- **Projected impact:** Property values up 15-20% in surrounding neighborhoods

### Major Development Projects

#### OMSI Redevelopment (Portland, 24 acres)
- **Scale:** 3 million square feet of new development
- **Components:** Restaurants, hotels, offices, retail, 1,200 apartments, Center for Tribal Nations
- **Timeline:** Groundbreaking expected late 2025

#### Housing Innovation Campus (Port of Portland, Marine Terminal 2)
- **Size:** 53-acre Marine Terminal 2
- **Focus:** Sustainable mass timber for modular housing
- **Economic impact:** 17,300 lumber/manufacturing jobs
- **Federal funding:** Recently awarded $41.4 million

### Regional Market Opportunities

#### Vancouver, WA - Emerging Investment Hub
- One of most promising markets for Washington investors

- Attracting young professionals/families seeking alternatives to Portland's costs

- **Affordable housing allocation:** $349.5 million (over 5x the 2019 spend)

### Regulatory Developments

### Market Implications
- Infrastructure investments creating long-term real estate growth catalysts

### Sales Activity Recovery
- Economists broadly expect rebound in 2026 after 2025 freeze

- Strong demand in select price bands

### Inventory & Supply Dynamics
- Homes for sale up 36.1% YoY

### Market Structure by Price Point
- Demand strong in several price bands

### Market Character
- Stabilized, more balanced market
- "Improvement without chaos" - orderly recovery
- Buyer and seller protections more balanced
- Less desperation on either side

- Early indicators strong - agents report busy spring

## Summary: Key Takeaways for Snohomish County Clients

### The Good News
1. **More inventory choice** for buyers after years of shortage
2. **Wage growth outpacing prices** for first time in years
3. **Mortgage rates lower** than 2024-2025, though above 6%
4. **Spring momentum building** with strong buyer activity
5. **Infrastructure investments** creating long-term growth catalysts

### Market Reality
1. **No longer seller's market** - conditions more balanced
2. **Price appreciation modest** - 2-5% expected (not 10-15% like pandemic era)
3. **Inventory still tight** by national standards (but better locally)
4. **Market stabilizing** after 2025 freeze

### Everett Opportunity
- Most affordable Snohomish County market ($600Ks vs. $700Ks county average)
- Quick sales (under 2 weeks common)
- Growing buyer interest as Seattle prices plateau
- Strong value proposition messaging

### Digital Marketing Priority
- AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) now critical for discoverability
- Voice search and mobile optimization non-negotiable
- Hyper-local neighborhood targeting more effective than city-wide
- Authenticity in content/video outperforms polish

### Agent Positioning
- Market moving from "oracle of supply" to "guide through choices"
- Buyer representation more valuable in balanced market
- Local expertise in neighborhoods increasingly valuable
- Educational content (AEO-optimized) attracts qualified leads
