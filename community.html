Kim's Commitment to the Pacific Northwest

More Than a Broker—A Neighbor

Kim isn't just selling homes in Snohomish, King, Pierce, and Skagit Counties—she's invested in these communities because they're where she's raised her family and built her life. Her deep roots in the Pacific Northwest go beyond business; they're personal.

From volunteering with local nonprofits to supporting neighborhood initiatives, Kim believes in giving back to the communities that have given her so much. She understands that selling or buying a home is deeply tied to the fabric of where you live—the schools, the parks, the coffee shops, and the people who make neighborhoods special.

This philosophy means she doesn't just market properties; she helps families find homes within communities they'll truly belong to.
