Two and a half hours. That was the plan.

We took the long way home

On Labor Day, we went for a scenic drive up Mount Finley, which is on the opposite side of the Skagit River from our property in Hamilton, Washington. It was a beautiful drive and we were able to spot our property from the mountaintop, which was really cool.

the top. before we knew what we were in for.

We decided to take a different road back, a loop along the back side of the mountain, but when we got a ways down that road, the road got very narrow and started to have areas where it was almost washed out. It was nerve-wracking, so we did a five-point turn and headed back the other way. We ended up taking the long way back, all along the back of Mount Finley and ended up near Concrete. What started out as a two and a half hour drive ended up being a 5-hour adventure!
