the sewer scope,

My buyer's sewer scope found a crack that another camera called tree roots.

My buyer is under contract on a 100-year-old house in Seattle, and on a house that age the sewer scope is the one inspection I never skip. Pricebusters Plumbing ran their camera down the old clay sewer line and found cracks in the pipe. Another company had scoped the same line and said the problem was tree roots. Pricebusters uses a high-resolution camera, and on their video you can clearly see the crack. They charge about $150 more than the other guys for a scope, and that $150 is the reason we knew what we were really dealing with!

Because we could show it was a crack, my buyer can ask the seller for a trenchless repair, which lines the old pipe with a polyurethane sleeve from the inside. Nobody has to dig up the hillside the house sits on, and that would have been a much bigger and more expensive job. I filmed the whole scope with the plumber, and I will share the video as soon as it is edited.

This is what the inspection period is for. You find out what you are really buying before you own it, and then you get to ask for the fix. We help you with this!
