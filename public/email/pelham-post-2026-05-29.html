Worth Knowing · May 2026

What "102.3%" actually means for your bottom line

The Snohomish County market is pricing at a 100.2% sale-to-list average right now (NWMLS). Translation: homes are selling for roughly what they list at. Not a feeding frenzy. Not a fire sale. A market that finally feels like a market.

My trailing 12-month sale-to-list sits at 102.3%. On a $750K Snohomish County home, that 2.1-point gap is about $15,750 more in the seller's pocket. On a $1M home, it's $21,000. The number comes from pricing strategy, prep, and not blinking in negotiation.

If you're thinking about listing this summer: the homes that win this market are the ones priced right on day one. The ones that price ego-first and chase reductions are the ones sitting.

Source: NWMLS monthly market reports, nwmls.com.
