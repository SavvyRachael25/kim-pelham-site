something new: community spotlight
I started a conversation series, and the first one changed how I think about moving
I sat down with Stephanie Galindo, an ADHD coach and an old friend, for the first conversation in my new Community Spotlight series. Stephanie used to live here in Snohomish County and now runs her coaching virtually from Alaska, so she knows the weight of a big move firsthand. No scripts, twenty minutes, just two friends talking.
Stephanie explained something I will never unhear. An ADHD brain spends years building pathways for daily life: which drawer, which route, which routine. A move erases all of them at once, and suddenly every small step takes active thinking. Her words: moving takes months, and settling back in can take up to a year. If a move ever left you living out of boxes and running on fumes, you were not failing. You were rebuilding every pathway at once.
She also taught me a nervous system trick you can do with a pen, and why packing with a friend in the room actually works. You can read the whole thing, or just press play and listen to the two of us talk.

what I am seeing
A calmer market, if you have been watching
There are more homes on the market right now than we have seen in a while. If you have felt priced out or just worn down by the bidding wars of the last few years, this is a gentler season to look. More choices, more time to think, less pressure to decide in a weekend.
And if you already own, this is a good moment to simply know where you stand. Not to do anything about it, just to know. I am always glad to talk it through with no agenda.
