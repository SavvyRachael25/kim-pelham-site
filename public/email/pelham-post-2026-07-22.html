Some weeks are about houses. This one is about a person.

For Joyce

Joyce Curman was both a dear friend and a valued client. I first met her through her daughter, Stephanie Larson, who was a member of my Heart to Heart networking group. When Joyce moved here after her retirement, she began attending our meetings with Stephanie. During our one-minute commercial segments, Joyce would introduce herself by fondly saying, "I'm Stephanie's mom, and I do nothing." We all quickly grew to love her as a second mom, and I was deeply honored when she chose me to sell her home.

Almost two years ago, Joyce was placed on hospice care. Deciding she wanted to actively enjoy her celebration of life, she hosted what she called her "Awake Wake." Her friends gathered, and she was able to experience an incredible outpouring of love. Defying the doctors' predictions, she subsequently got off hospice and continued to live fully for another two years.

It is a beautiful testament to Joyce and her family how everyone gathered around her in her final days, with many traveling across the country to say goodbye to their beloved mother and grandmother. I am so impressed by the support her family provided and the beautiful way they helped her transition from this earth. I am truly honored to have called her a friend.

What the market is actually doing

Inventory across Snohomish County has climbed since the spring. There are simply more homes to choose from than there were a few months ago, and prices have softened off their peak. For anyone who spent the last two years feeling boxed out, that is the news: you have room to look, room to think, and room to negotiate again.

If you are selling, this does not mean waiting. It means pricing honestly and preparing the home properly, which is exactly the part I handle for you. Well-prepared homes are still selling close to ask. The ones sitting are the ones that got priced on last year's optimism.

lucy's corner

You have started seeing a certain someone in our videos. That is Lucy, our office dog and self-appointed Director of First Impressions. She has opinions about real estate, and honestly, some of them are pretty good.

Lucy's tip this month: the market moved, so the smart buyers are circling back to houses that sat. A home that did not sell in June is often a better deal in July, and the seller is a lot more willing to talk. Lucy calls this sniffing out the good ones. I call it paying attention.
 

pick of the week

Makario Coffee Roasters

If you have not been to Makario on Colby, go. It is a small, independent roaster right here in Everett, and the name means "how blessed you are." Which, this week of all weeks, felt like the right note to end on. They roast their own beans, the Dutch cold brew is worth the wait, and it has quietly become one of the best gathering spots downtown. Support the local folks. That is the whole point of a place like this.
