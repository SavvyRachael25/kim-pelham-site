Here’s how your business can get found by AI.

Many of you run businesses. Here are some non-technical, free things you can do to help AI recommend you to potential clients.

1. Say the same thing everywhere.

Your name, address, phone number and the same short bio should be identical on every site you appear on, from Google and Yelp to your industry directories and your author page. When two sources disagree about your address or how long you have been in business, that disagreement is the reason you get left off the list.

Here's a link to the bio that is the same everywhere from Amazon to Zillow: View My Bio on Amazon

2. Ask for reviews, and answer them.

Google reviews are the biggest single input. Ask with a direct link that opens the review box, rather than sending people to your website and hoping they find it. Answer every review, because a page where the owner replies reads differently than a page where nobody does.

Here's my direct link for Google reviews:

Leave Kim a Review

3. Get Your Business Listed on Other Websites.

AI can find information about your business on websites other than your own. Two good places to start are business directories and articles that feature local businesses.

Business directories are websites like Google Business Profile, Yelp, and Zillow, where customers can find information about your business. Make sure your listings are accurate and up to date.

Articles and “Best Of” lists are another way to get noticed. Think of articles like “10 Best Realtors in Snohomish County” or local awards like “Best of Snohomish County,” which I've been fortunate enough to win twice!

Look for opportunities to be featured in local publications, community spotlights, and industry articles. These independent mentions can help establish your credibility and give AI more information about your business.

4. Answer the questions people actually ask.

Add a Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) section to your website. Use the questions customers ask you every day and answer them clearly in everyday language. AI can use these answers when recommending your business to potential customers.
