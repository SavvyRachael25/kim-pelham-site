I am hosting on The American Dream TV

It is a lifestyle show about the people and places that make a community, and it has been nominated for two Emmys and won a Telly. This fall I am filming the Snohomish County segments.

Here is the part I keep thinking about. I get to pick what we film. Not a producer in another state who has never driven Colby Avenue. Me. Seventeen years of showing people around this county, and now there is a camera crew attached to it.

Which is where I need you, because I do not know every good story out here. You do.
