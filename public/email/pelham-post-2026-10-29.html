how to get found that way yourself,

Plenty of you run businesses. None of this is technical and none of it costs anything.

Say the same thing everywhere.

Your name, address, phone number and the same short bio should be identical on every site you appear on, from Google and Yelp to your industry directories and your author page. When two sources disagree about your address or how long you have been in business, that disagreement is the reason you get left off the list.

Ask for reviews, and answer them.

Google reviews are the biggest single input. Ask with a direct link that opens the review box, rather than sending people to your website and hoping they find it. Answer every review, because a page where the owner replies reads differently than a page where nobody does.

Get on the lists that already exist.

Ask an assistant for the best plumber in Everett and it mostly reads roundups and directories, not individual websites. Being strong on those sites matters more than trying to outrank them. Most have a free profile sitting empty with your name on it.

Answer the questions people actually ask.

Write the plain answer to the thing customers ask you on the phone every week, in the words they use to ask it. That is what gets quoted back. Marketing language does not get quoted.
