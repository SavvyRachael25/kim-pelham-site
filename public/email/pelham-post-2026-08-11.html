creating a playbook for expired listings

What can be done when a house doesn't sell the first time around?

I was contacted recently by homeowners in Bothell whose home didn't sell this spring. I engaged a two-pronged plan to revamp the listing: a scientific approach and an energy check.

First, I examined the existing listing's data, positioning and photos to check for errors and anything that might be limiting showings. We improved the lighting upstairs and set up sprinklers on a timer to water the lawn.

Next, I checked the energy of the house - how welcoming does it feel? Will a buyer become intrigued when they see the photos and emotionally engaged when they tour the home? To accomplish this, I staged the home extensively and worked with a real estate energy specialist to shift its energy.

Do you know someone whose home didn't sell the first time around? My Expired Listing Process may be just the ticket.
