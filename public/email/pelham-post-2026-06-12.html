Writing you from a different desk this week.

We moved. After weeks of waiting on the lease, the keys finally landed and Brien and I are settling into the new spot in Everett. Same county, same coffee order, slightly fewer boxes by the hour. I will send the full new address as soon as the sign is on the door, but the work has not skipped a beat. Phones still ring to me, email still lands the same place, and we are showing and listing on the same schedule we always do.

Moving is humbling, even when you do it for a living. You pack up a corner you have stood in for years and suddenly you see how much of it was just memory holding things in place. New room, same plan. Walking alongside our buyers and sellers, one home at a time.

speaking of fresh starts,

Who doesn't love a before and after story?

I have been wanting to tell you about this one for a while. Two homeowners moved out of state mid-prep. The condo was already a wonderful home, full of bright color and personality. But selling from across the country meant they could not be the ones swinging by the paint store on a Tuesday afternoon. So they handed the keys to us.

Over the next several weeks, we handled the whole prep. Paint, new flooring, hardware, refinishing the surfaces that needed a second look, lighting tuned up, and then a full staging and a professional listing-photo shoot. No round trips for the sellers. No coordinating across time zones with three different vendors. One crew, one project manager (that part is me), one set of expectations.

Sixty-four photos tell the story better than I can. The bright turquoise kids' bedroom became a soft, neutral retreat. The salmon-and-teal guest bath got a calmer hand. The living room, with its navy accent and white-tile fireplace, opened up into a sage and marble-herringbone moment that holds the whole room. It is a real before and after, and the unit is on the market right now.
