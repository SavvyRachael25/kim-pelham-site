the numbers,

Rates went up. Here's what it costs.

Freddie Mac's 30-year rate came in at 6.95% on Thursday, September 17. It was 6.76% the week before. That's the highest since January 2025.

On the median Snohomish County home ($724,500 in August, per NWMLS) with 20% down, the payment went from $3,763 a month to $3,837. Principal and interest only. About $74 a month, $890 a year, in one week.

If you were pre-approved over the summer, call your lender and get the number refreshed before you write an offer. If you're selling, this is one more reason your buyer is careful right now.
