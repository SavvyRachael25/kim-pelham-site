the numbers,

"Rates went up. What does that do to my payment?"

Freddie Mac's 30-year rate came in at 6.95% on Thursday, September 17. It was 6.76% the week before, and it is the highest since January 2025.

On the median Snohomish County home ($724,500 in August, per NWMLS) with 20% down, the principal and interest payment went from $3,763 a month to $3,837. That is $74 a month, about $890 a year, in one week.

My recommendations:

If you are buying and were pre-approved over the summer, call your lender and get the number refreshed before you write an offer. It is possible rates come back down this fall. BUT, I would not plan a purchase around a rate that might happen. If you are selling, this is one more reason your buyer is careful, and a seller credit that buys their rate down sometimes gets a deal done that a price cut would not.

Where we are at: 39% more homes for sale than a year ago, rates at a 20-month high, buyers with choices and a calculator. Sellers who prepare and price well are still selling.
