the numbers,

Rates went up this week.

Freddie Mac's 30-year rate hit 6.95% on Thursday. It was 6.76% the week before. That's the highest it has been since January 2025.

What does that mean in real money? On a $724,500 house (that's the county median) with 20% down, the payment went from $3,763 to $3,837 a month. About $74 more a month, or $890 a year. Not huge, but not nothing.

If you got pre-approved over the summer, call your lender and get a fresh number before you write an offer. If you are selling, this is one more reason buyers are being careful right now. A seller credit that buys down the buyer's rate can sometimes get a deal done when a price cut won't.
