Yes, you can live in your home and sell it at the same time. Here is how to do it without losing your mind.

Living in a Staged Home

Living in a staged home while it’s on the market can feel overwhelming, especially when you’re juggling work, family and an upcoming move. But keeping your home show-ready doesn’t have to mean constantly cleaning.

The secret is developing a few simple habits that take less than a minute: make the bed when you get up, put clothes directly into the hamper or closet, keep personal-care items in an easy-to-store basket, and reset each room as you leave it. Then, when a showing is scheduled, you’re not facing a major cleanup, just a quick final check.

I put together a practical Living in a Staged Home guide with easy, realistic tips and estimated times for each task (most take under a minute).
