Monday, I point a camera at 175 women.

The first segment films Monday October 12 at 100+ Women Who Care About Snohomish County, at Normanna Lodge in Everett. Here is how that group works: about 175 women show up, everyone puts in $100, three nonprofits get a few minutes to make their case, the room votes, and one of them walks out with the whole pot. No staff, no overhead, nobody takes a cut.

Since 2019 they have given $505,856 to 21 Snohomish County nonprofits. Janet Bacon and Susan Riake started it. The Herald wrote them up in August.

the numbers,

7.28%, and the sixth week in a row.

Freddie Mac's 30-year average came in at 7.28% on Thursday. It was 7.03% the week before, and it has gone up six weeks running. A year ago this week it was 6.34%.

On a $724,500 house, which is the county median, with 20% down: the payment is now about $3,966 a month. Last week it was $3,868. That is $98 more a month after one week.

The number that actually matters is the year. The same house at last October's rate was $3,603 a month. It is $363 more now, which is $4,356 a year for the identical house, the identical price, the identical loan.

That is the whole reason buyers are so careful right now, and it is why I spent a paragraph last week on who pays the buyer's agent.
