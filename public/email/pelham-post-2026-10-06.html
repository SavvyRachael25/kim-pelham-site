the numbers,

7.28%, and why that is not the whole story.

Freddie Mac's 30-year average came in at 7.28% on Thursday, up from 7.03%, the sixth weekly increase in a row. A year ago it was 6.34%.

On a $724,500 house, the county median, with 20% down, the payment is about $3,966 a month. The same house at last October's rate was $3,603. That is $4,356 a year more for an identical house at an identical price, and I am not going to pretend that is nothing.

Here is the part that does not make the headlines. In March of 2024 there were 424 active homes for sale in Snohomish County. Today there are 3,071. Everyone who got scared off by the rate left the field, and what they left behind is choice.

So the house that would have drawn eleven offers in 2022 sits. You can ask for repairs. You can ask for closing costs. You can ask the seller to buy your rate down, and plenty of them will, because the alternative is another month of carrying it. Sellers are paying the buyer's agent again too. I just put that in writing on my own listing in Bothell.

The rate is the worst part of buying right now. It is also the only reason you are not standing in line.
