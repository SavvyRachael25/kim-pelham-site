the thing almost nobody knows,

My buyers just got 5.125% with a loan payment less than their rent.

Everybody else will be signing at 7.49% this week. These first-time buyers did not have to buy down their interest rate. They took over the seller's existing loan. What's interesting about this transaction is that this home was not advertised as having an assumable loan. I inquired about the type of loan, learning it was VA, and then created the opportunity for my buyers to assume the loan.

If a seller has an FHA, VA or USDA loan, a buyer can step into it at the rate the seller got instead of today's rate. Conventional loans cannot be assumed.

For my buyers, their house payment will be $2756 instead of $3100 on a $455,000 home. Their current rent is $3000, so they will be buying a house for less than their monthly rent!

How a loan assumption works: The buyers have to pay the difference between the purchase price of $455,000 and the loan balance of $407,000 which equals $48,000. They had $60k for a down payment, so this totally fit their budget.

So, the catch to loan assumptions is finding assumable loans that have a balance that fits the buyer's down payment budget. A bonus is that the buyer will save thousands on loan origination fees, so they may have more buying power than they thought.

the numbers,

Rates are at 7.49%. Wow.

Freddie Mac's 30-year average came in at 7.49% today (10/5/26), up from 7.03%, the sixth weekly increase in a row. A year ago it was 6.34%.

On a $724,500 house, the county median, with 20% down, the payment is about $3,966 a month. The same house at last October's rate was $3,603. That is $4,356 a year more for the same house at the same price.

There are seven times as many homes to choose from as there were in March of 2024.

Here is the part that does not make the headlines. In March of 2024 there were 424 active homes for sale in Snohomish County, and today there are 3,071. A lot of buyers got scared off by the rate and stepped back, and what they left behind for everyone else is choice.

That means the house that would have drawn eleven offers in 2022 is sitting on the market now, and you are in a position to ask for concessions. You can ask for repairs, help with closing costs, and you can ask the seller to buy your rate down, which plenty of them will do because they fear the house will never sell.

The rate is the hardest part of buying right now, but it is also the reason you are not standing in line behind ten other buyers, risking everything buying without an inspection! Yikes! Those were scary times for buyers.

Remember too that Washington homebuyers may have access to more than 50 down payment assistance programs. I can help you see which you qualify for.

I have a policy of working with a maximum of two active buyers at a time, which means that when you text me on a Saturday about a house you drove past, I am available to answer your questions and book a showing if you want to see the inside.
