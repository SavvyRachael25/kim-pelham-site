if you have been waiting to buy,

Nobody is bidding against you right now.

High rates scared most buyers off and the houses piled up behind them, which is miserable if you are selling and quietly very good if you are not. It means you can ask for things that nobody was in a position to ask for three years ago, and if this is your first house, there is help in this state that most people never hear about.

the numbers,

Rates are at 7.28%, and that is not the whole story.

Freddie Mac's 30-year average came in at 7.28% on Thursday, up from 7.03%, the sixth weekly increase in a row. A year ago it was 6.34%.

On a $724,500 house, the county median, with 20% down, the payment is about $3,966 a month. The same house at last October's rate was $3,603. That is $4,356 a year more for the same house at the same price, and I am not going to pretend that is nothing.

There are seven times as many homes to choose from as there were in March of 2024.

Here is the part that does not make the headlines. In March of 2024 there were 424 active homes for sale in Snohomish County, and today there are 3,071. A lot of buyers got scared off by the rate and stepped back, and what they left behind for everyone else is choice.

That means the house that would have drawn eleven offers in 2022 is sitting on the market now, and you are in a position to ask for things. You can ask for repairs, you can ask for help with closing costs, and you can ask the seller to buy your rate down, which plenty of them will do because the alternative is carrying the house for another month. Sellers are paying the buyer's agent again too, and I just put that in writing on my own listing in Bothell.

The rate is the hardest part of buying right now, but it is also the reason you are not standing in line behind ten other buyers.

If this is your first house, all of that goes double, because there is down payment help in Washington that most people never hear about until somebody sits down with them and explains it.

One thing you should know before you write to me is that I take two active buyers at a time, which is a policy I do not bend for anybody. It means that when you text me on a Saturday about a house you drove past, I already know your situation well enough to tell you something useful in a sentence.
 
 
 

the thing almost nobody knows,

My buyers just got 5.125%.

Everybody else is signing at 7.28% this week. These were first-time buyers, and they did not use a special program or know somebody on the inside. They took over the loan the seller already had.

It is called an assumption. If a seller has an FHA, VA or USDA loan, a buyer can step into it at the rate the seller got instead of today's rate. Conventional loans cannot be assumed, which is why most people have never heard of this.

Here is what that gap is worth on the county median house with 20% down. The payment is $3,966 a month at 7.28%, or $3,156 at 5.125%. That is a difference of about $810 every month, or just under $9,700 a year, and that kind of money changes what a household can do.

There is a catch, and I will not pretend otherwise. You assume the balance, not the price, so you have to cover the difference between what the seller still owes and what you are paying them. That is the part that stops most of these, and it is the first thing I check.

the part nobody else can do,

Brien is my secret weapon.

I say that about my husband all the time, and I mean it as a business fact as much as a sweet one. With this many houses sitting, a lot of what you walk through is dated. You will see brown cabinets, bathrooms nobody has touched since 1988, and kitchens somebody started and never finished. Most buyers look at that and either walk away or guess at a number, and guessing is how people get into trouble.

He is a general contractor, and his company SMART Building Services has been the other half of how we work for years. So when you are standing in a house wondering whether that wall moves, or how much roof is left, or what the kitchen really costs, you are not asking me to guess, you are asking him. He will give you a real number before you write the offer, not after.

We do the same thing on the other side for sellers who are getting a house ready to compete with three thousand others. It is the same team working in the opposite direction.
