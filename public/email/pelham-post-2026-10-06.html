if you have been waiting to buy,

Nobody is bidding against you right now.

High rates scared most buyers off, and the houses piled up. That is miserable if you are selling. If you are buying, it means you can ask for things nobody could ask for three years ago. First house? Especially then. There is down payment help in this state that most people never hear about.

Hit reply and tell me what you are looking for. It comes straight to me, not to a form.
 

the numbers,

7.28%, and why that is not the whole story.

Freddie Mac's 30-year average came in at 7.28% on Thursday, up from 7.03%, the sixth weekly increase in a row. A year ago it was 6.34%.

On a $724,500 house, the county median, with 20% down, the payment is about $3,966 a month. The same house at last October's rate was $3,603. That is $4,356 a year more for an identical house at an identical price, and I am not going to pretend that is nothing.

seven times the choice.

Here is the part that does not make the headlines. In March of 2024 there were 424 active homes for sale in Snohomish County. Today there are 3,071. Everyone who got scared off by the rate left the field, and what they left behind is choice.

So the house that would have drawn eleven offers in 2022 sits. You can ask for repairs. You can ask for closing costs. You can ask the seller to buy your rate down, and plenty of them will, because the alternative is another month of carrying it. Sellers are paying the buyer's agent again too. I just put that in writing on my own listing in Bothell.

The rate is the worst part of buying right now. It is also the only reason you are not standing in line.

If this is your first house, that goes double, and there is down payment help in Washington that most people never hear about until someone tells them.

One thing you should know before you write to me: I take two active buyers at a time. That is a policy and I do not bend it. It means when you text me on a Saturday about a house you drove past, I already know your situation and I can tell you something useful in a sentence.
 
 

the part nobody else can do,

Brien is my secret weapon.

I say that about my husband and I mean it commercially, not sweetly. With this many houses sitting, a lot of what you are going to walk through is dated. Brown cabinets. A bathroom from 1988. A kitchen somebody started and did not finish. Most buyers see that and either run or guess at a number, and guessing is how people get hurt.

He is a general contractor. SMART Building Services is his company, and it has been the other half of how we work for years. So when you are standing in a house wondering whether that wall moves, whether the roof has five years left in it, or what that kitchen actually costs to do properly, you are not asking me to guess. You are asking him, and he will give you a real number before you write the offer, not after.

That is also what we do on the other side, for sellers getting a house ready to compete with three thousand other ones. Same team, opposite direction.

and one good thing,

It closed.

37 acres, south of Olympia. Lucy supervised.

Someone referred a buyer to me for 37 acres in Tenino, which is south of Olympia, with tiny homes on it. Not a house. Land.

Brien did the feasibility work, researching what the buyer could actually do with the property before anyone signed anything, and we drove down and walked the whole thing with him. That is the part I want you to hear, because it is the same question whether it is 37 acres or a 1,240 square foot rambler: what can you actually do with this, and what will it cost you to find out. Somebody should answer that before you buy it, not after.

It closed this week. A good one to end the month on.
