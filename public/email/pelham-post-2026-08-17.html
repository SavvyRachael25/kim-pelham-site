The market just handed buyers something they have not had in a while: choices.

The Snohomish County market just changed

The Snohomish County real estate market just changed, and both buyers and sellers should be paying attention.

In July, the number of homes for sale jumped 36.6% compared with last year, giving buyers something they haven't had much of lately: choices. Homes are also taking longer to sell, averaging 28 days on the market compared with 25 days last year.

Buyers are taking more time, comparing homes and becoming less willing to overlook condition or an ambitious price.

For sellers, that doesn't mean it's a bad time to sell. It means pricing and preparation matter more than ever. Well-priced, beautifully presented homes can still attract buyers quickly. Others may sit.

For buyers, more inventory means more opportunity to negotiate, and potentially less competition.
