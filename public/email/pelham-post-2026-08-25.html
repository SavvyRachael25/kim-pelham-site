It is the question I hear every year about this time: should I sell this fall, or wait until spring?

Should I Sell Now or Wait for Spring?

Spring traditionally brings more buyers, but it also brings a lot more competition. Here in Snohomish County, the number of new listings historically begins dropping in September and falls substantially by November.

That can create an opportunity for fall sellers. Serious buyers don't disappear when summer ends. People still relocate, change jobs, get married, downsize and need more (or less) space. And with fewer new homes coming on the market, a well-prepared and correctly priced home may have less competition.

This year adds another consideration: inventory is already substantially higher than it was last year, giving buyers more choices. Waiting until spring could mean entering the market alongside another wave of sellers.

On the other hand, spring may make sense if your home needs significant repairs, landscaping or preparation that will improve its value, or if selling now simply doesn't fit your plans.

The better question isn't "Is fall or spring better?" It's "Which market gives your particular home the best opportunity?"

If you're considering selling in the next six months, now is a good time to look at your home, your neighborhood and your likely competition and make that decision based on the numbers, not the season.
