What people ask before they call me

Honest answers.

Do I really pay nothing upfront?

Yes. For qualifying homes, I front the cost of the agreed updates and staging. It’s repaid from your proceeds at closing — nothing comes out of your pocket along the way.

What if my home doesn’t need much?

Then I’ll tell you that. Sometimes the right move is paint and a deep clean, not a remodel. The Playbook helps you see the difference before you spend a dollar.

Is this just for big, expensive homes?

No. The biggest returns I see are often on modest homes that simply photograph and show better after a focused, affordable prep.

What’s the catch with the free guide?

There isn’t one. I keep two clients at a time, so I’d rather start a real relationship than chase a quick sale. Read it, use it, call me if it’s helpful.
