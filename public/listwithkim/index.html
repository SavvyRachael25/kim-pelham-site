What people ask before they call me

Honest answers.

Can I pay for the work at closing?

If you’d rather not pay as we go, yes — for qualifying homes, financing is available so the cost of the agreed updates and staging can be settled from your proceeds at closing. It’s an option, not the only way; many sellers simply pay for the prep directly.

What if my home doesn’t need much?

Then I’ll tell you that. Sometimes the right move is paint and a deep clean, not a remodel. The Playbook helps you see the difference before you spend a dollar.

Is this just for big, expensive homes?

No. The biggest returns I see are often on modest homes that simply photograph and show better after a focused, affordable prep.

What’s the catch with the free guide?

There isn’t one. I keep two buyers at a time, so I’d rather start a real relationship than chase a quick sale. Read it, use it, call me if it’s helpful.
