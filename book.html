About The Book

About Kim Pelham

Kim Pelham is a best-selling author and renowned real estate broker with The Pelham Group NW, operating under Katrina Eileen Brokerage in Everett, Washington.

With 14 years of real estate experience - plus three years in commercial property management - Kim has guided families through the selling process with confidence. Her unique approach combines psychology-driven staging, data-backed marketing, and genuine care for her clients' outcomes.

Kim's success is reflected in her results: a 104.4% sale-to-list ratio and a commitment to individualized service that large teams simply can't match.

When she's not working with clients, Kim enjoys spending time with her family. Her resilience, compassion, and commitment to excellence shine through in everything she does.
