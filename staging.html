Tale of Two Brokers

A homeowner in Snohomish County listed their property with a traditional broker — professional photos, a Zillow listing, and a sign in the yard. Months went by. Showings trickled in, but no offers materialized. The price seemed right based on comps, but the home just sat there.

The Challenge: The listing looked like every other house on the market. Buyers walked through and saw the home's flaws instead of its potential. No emotional connection. No urgency. The broker's strategy was "list it and wait."

Frustrated, the homeowner came to Kim Pelham for a second opinion. Kim walked the property and saw what the previous broker missed: the home had incredible bones, but the presentation was working against it. Worn carpet distracted from the open floor plan. Cluttered rooms made spaces feel smaller. The curb appeal didn't match the neighborhood.

Kim's team went to work — coordinating targeted repairs, professional staging that highlighted the home's best features, and a marketing strategy designed to create urgency. The transformation was dramatic. Within two weeks, the home had multiple competing offers and sold for $10,000 above the listing price in just 5 days.

The Difference: It wasn't luck. It was psychology-driven staging paired with a proven marketing system. The first broker listed a house. Kim sold a lifestyle. Buyers walked in and could see themselves living there — and they were willing to pay more for that feeling.

Key Takeaways for Home Sellers

Select Your Broker Carefully

Your broker's strategy and resources directly impact your home's success. Work with someone who invests in staging and marketing.

Ask About Selling Strategy

Before listing, understand how your broker will position and promote your home. Professional staging is part of a winning strategy.

Staging Results in Higher Offers

In Kim's experience, staging delivers up to a 300% return on investment. The investment in staging pays for itself — and then some.
