Our goal as your realtor is to help you get the maximum money for your home while minimizing the stress and time it takes to sell.

Currently, our average list-to-sale price is 104.4% versus the 97.98% Seattle average — consistently getting sellers more than asking price.

That kind of performance translates to real dollars. On a $500,000 home, the difference between a 104.4% and a 97.98% sale-to-list ratio is over $32,000 more in your pocket.

Our 6-Step Proven System

1

Identify Repairs

We leverage our contractor background and expertise to conduct a thorough inspection. This identifies both necessary repairs and strategic improvements that will maximize your home's market value. Through our Concierge program, many repairs can be completed at no upfront cost — you pay at closing.

2

Staging: Assessment

We walk through your home and recommend what to keep, what to remove, and what needs attention. We identify problem areas that could deter buyers and provide clear guidance on making your home shine.

3

Staging: Execution

The week before your showing begins, we arrange professional staging. This transforms your home into a buyer's dream, helping them envision their future in your space. Our staging and home repair services work hand-in-hand — we coordinate everything so you don't have to.

4

Professional Photography

First impressions matter. We provide professional, magazine-quality photography that showcases your home in its best light and attracts serious buyers online.

5

Online Marketing

Your listing is syndicated across every major real estate platform and social media channel for maximum exposure. We ensure qualified buyers find your home quickly.

6

Quality Marketing Materials

We create professionally printed materials, brochures, and direct mail campaigns. These tangible marketing pieces keep your home top-of-mind for buyers and agents.

The Selling Process

Strategic Price Positioning

Before we list your home, we may recommend a strategic price adjustment based on current market conditions. This positions your home competitively to generate multiple offers and maximize your final sale price.

Expert Guidance at Every Step

We don't just list homes—we guide you through every step. From negotiation strategies to understanding inspection reports, we're here to make informed decisions easier and less stressful.

Multiple Offer Mastery

When multiple buyers want your home, we skillfully navigate negotiations. We analyze offers beyond price, considering terms, contingencies, and timelines to secure the best overall deal for you.

Closing with Confidence

Once an offer is accepted, we manage inspections, appraisals, and closing logistics. We stay on top of every detail so you can focus on your next adventure with confidence.
